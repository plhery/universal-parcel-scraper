# ANT Logistics & Forwarding

ANT Logistics & Forwarding parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ANT Logistics & Forwarding (ANT A.E.) is a logistics and forwarding firm in Greece that also
delivers parcels in Attica within 15 or 24 hours. Its site is ant-vl.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows an ANT Logistics & Forwarding number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page posts a shipment number from a script and shows the answer in the page. With no
public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
