# Fixtures

Tracking pages for the public sample in `numbers.json`, cut down to the search box, the step
bar, the status card and the history; the site's menus, styles, scripts and icons are left out.

`2236371763054025.html` is the page the browser rendered once the package was collected.
`2236371763054025-sorted.html` is the archived page from before, sorted at the destination
office with its expected delivery day. `not-found.html` is the page for an impossible id.
