Replies of AirAsia's SmartKargo tracker, opened from its `AWBPrefix` and `AWBNo` query, with scripts,
styles and the ASP.NET form state removed.

- `departed.html`: the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), an Indonesia AirAsia
  shipment from Bali to Balikpapan whose last milestone is its departure.
- `not-found.html`: the answer for an invented AirAsia waybill with a valid check digit.
