Replies of the public Dutch tracking service for the public samples in `numbers.json`:

- `delivered.json` and `lost-after-attempt.json`: live replies of
  `https://dragonflyshipping.nl/cfworker/v3/tracking/<number>/`.
- `archived-back-to-depot.json`: the reply the Wayback Machine archived from the service's older
  `v2` route, which puts the short label on the status itself.
- `not-found.json`: the service's reply to an invented number.
