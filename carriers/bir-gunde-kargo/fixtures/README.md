`out-for-delivery.html` is the tracker's page for a public sample barcode in `numbers.json`, with the courier's and the
branch staff's names, the courier's photograph, the vehicle plate, the branch's street, map position and extension, and
the recipient's masked name, address and phone replaced. `completed.html` is its page for the public sample order number
of a finished parcel. `not-found.html` is its answer to a synthetic barcode.
