# Best Courier

Best Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Best Courier is a courier in Bucharest that delivers with its own vehicles and local agents under its
own AWB numbers. Its site is bestcourier.ro.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Best Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page, hosted on CourierManager, a courier software platform, takes an AWB through a form and shows its scans. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
