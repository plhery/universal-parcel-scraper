Synthetic replies of DealerSend's tracking lookup, shaped as its live replies: `delivered-usps.json`
is a parcel USPS delivered after a US partner, `delivered-gofo.json` one GOFO delivered, and
`in-transit-canada.json` one out for delivery in Canada with a partner number no carrier is named
for. `not-found.json` is the answer for a number the tracker does not know. Numbers and clocks are
invented, places are towns and airports with a placeholder where the tracker writes a ZIP code,
and the wording is the tracker's.
