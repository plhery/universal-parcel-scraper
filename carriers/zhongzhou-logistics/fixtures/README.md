`track.json` is a synthetic reply of Zhongzhou Logistics' Hualei track interface, in the tenant's
shape: the order row, the Shenzhen warehouse, the flight, French customs and the hand-over to the
delivery carrier, with the tracker's Chinese and English twin rows and GOFO's label row copied
twice. Numbers, references, the product and clocks are invented. `no-track.json` is the interface's
answer for a number it holds no row for, and `page-unknown.html` the tracking page's real answer for
the invented number `ZZ9999999999CN`. The tracker answers in GBK; the files are UTF-8.
