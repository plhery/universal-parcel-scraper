# Fixtures

Pages of Express One Bulgaria's English tracking page, `expressone.bg/en/tracking`.

- `tracking-page.html`: the page before a lookup, with the form and its token.
- `delivered.html`: the page the form returns for a shipment delivered at an office after a
  failed attempt, with a row the page lists twice and the pickup office as a link.
- `not-found.html`: the page the form returns for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers, the token, offices, towns and dates are invented.
