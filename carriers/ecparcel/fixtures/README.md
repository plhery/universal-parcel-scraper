Replies of ecParcel's `GET /api/shipmentTracking/getShipmentTracking`. `YOTN20260423100000003.json`
is the whole reply to the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), unchanged: it names
suburbs and no person. `not-found.json` is the answer, HTTP 404, for a synthetic number of the
sample's shape dated in 2099. The tests derive their other replies from the sample.
