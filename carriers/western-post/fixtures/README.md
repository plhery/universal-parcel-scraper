# Fixtures

Replies of Western Post's tracker, `www.wpglb.com/trackQuery`, posted one number as the home page's tracking box
posts it.

- `delivered.json`: the reply for the public sample in `numbers.json`, a parcel taken from the warehouse in Walnut
  to Western Post's distribution center in Ontario, California, and delivered in Indio. It names no person: the
  delivery photo it counts is shown only for the signer's name.
- `not-found.json`: the reply for a synthetic number of the sample's shape that the tracker does not know.
