# SWISS WorldCargo

SWISS WorldCargo shipments use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

SWISS WorldCargo, the cargo division of Swiss International Air Lines, carries air freight on SWISS's flights from
Zurich and Geneva.

## Numbers

Its air waybills are the prefix 724 and eight digits, a shape every airline shares, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The Search & Track box of its Offer and Order portal finds an air waybill without an account. With no public number to check the tracker's replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracker takes the number only from its form, so no address opens one shipment.
