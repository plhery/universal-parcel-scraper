# Emole Express

Emole Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Emole Express is a freight and express operator in Warsaw, Poland, with its own hub near Warsaw and
lines to Germany, Spain, Ukraine and the United Kingdom, and first and last-mile delivery for online
shops. Its site is emole-express.com.

## Numbers

Its tracking page asks for a 10-digit shipment number. No public page shows a whole number, so there
is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows a shipment's status, its current place and a timeline of events with place,
date and time. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens a number from its `number` parameter and searches at once when `autoSearch`
is true.
