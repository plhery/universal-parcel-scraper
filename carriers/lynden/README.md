# Lynden

Lynden shipments use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Lynden's companies carry freight to and within Alaska by truck, barge and air, Lynden Transport, Alaska Marine Lines and
Lynden Air Cargo among them.

## Numbers

Its tracking numbers are six to eight digits, a shape many carriers share, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking center's form opens Lynden's shipment trace. With no public number to check the tracker's replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The form opens `ezc.lynden.com/tracking/?webctl=read.traceio&everybody=ON&lnbr=<number>`.
