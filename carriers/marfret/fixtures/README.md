`found-*.xml` and `notfound-*.xml` are the tracker's replies to its container search for public
samples in `numbers.json` and for the invented unknown, named by the container.
`page-*.xml` is its reply for a later page of a sample's moves, named by the container and the
first row. `expired.xml` is its reply to a search posting a view it no longer holds, and
`search.html` the forms of its page. Each reply's view is replaced by a synthetic one, and the
page keeps its forms without Cloudflare's scripts. The tests edit them for the cases no sample
shows.
