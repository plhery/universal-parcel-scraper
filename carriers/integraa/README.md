# Integraa

Integraa items use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Integraa (Integraa Holding s.r.l., Integraa Posta) is a private postal operator in Rome and
Giugliano in Campania. It delivers registered letters, mail with a certified date and time, and
judicial acts under a special licence. Its site is integraaposta.it.

## Numbers

No public page shows an Integraa barcode or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page at `integraa.net/gestione/tracking.php` enables its search button only once the
visitor has solved a reCAPTCHA. Its service charter says the page shows every step from
acceptance to the last delivery attempt.

## Links

The search needs the reCAPTCHA, so no address opens one item.
