# KTD

KTD parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

KTD is 深圳市跨通达物流有限公司 (跨通达物流), a Shenzhen cross-border forwarder founded in 2016
with FBA lines, dedicated small-parcel lines and air freight.

## Numbers

Its parcels carry KTDWE and ten digits, the shape a universal provider files under KTD. On
its US line the number is also the tracking number at DHLink, which hands the parcel to SwiftX,
and DHLink's lookup finds it. So the rule only suggests KTD, after DHLink: recognition asks
DHLink, and a parcel DHLink does not know waits for a provider to name KTD. No tracker of
跨通达 confirms the shape. 快通达国际快递 also goes by KTD, but its own numbers are KTD and ten
digits, without WE. No public number has been found, so the corpus holds a synthetic one.

## Links

No tracking page of the carrier remains, so no link opens one parcel.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page of its site, `kuatongda.cn/chaxun.html`, only sends the number to an
aggregator through a relay on another domain, and that domain no longer resolves. Its older
site at `kuatongda.com` is gone. KTD publishes no app, and the QR code on its site adds a
customer-service contact, not a service account. No tenant of the Kingtrans, 易抵达, NextSLS or
RTB forwarder platforms carries its name. The order search at `ktdexpress.com` is
快通达国际快递's, whose own numbers are KTD and ten digits. So KTD stays on universal providers.
