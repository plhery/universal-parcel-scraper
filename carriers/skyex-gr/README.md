# Skyex Bioex

Skyex Bioex parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Skyex Bioex is a courier in Thessaloniki and Athens, Greece, that carries documents and parcels
between the two cities within hours, with partners for the rest of Greece and abroad. Its BIOEX
service carries biological samples. Its site is skyex.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Skyex Bioex number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page runs on Pegasus Hermes, the courier software behind
[Pack-Man](../pack-man/README.md)'s former voucher search, and shows vouchers, several separated by
commas, with their status, the partner's voucher when another courier delivers, the recipient and
the delivery date. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The voucher search opens numbers from its `r18p01` parameter.
