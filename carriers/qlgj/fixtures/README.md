Synthetic replies of Qilin International's Hualei track interface, in the shape of live ones, with
made-up numbers, ids and dates. `track.json` is a parcel on the economy line to France, which DHL
delivers, arrived at Amsterdam. `unknown.json` is the interface's answer for a made-up number it
does not know. The interface's replies are GBK; the files are UTF-8.
