# Happy Courier

Happy Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Happy Courier is a courier in Athens, Greece, that delivers under its own waybills, called ΣΥ.ΔΕ.ΤΑ.
Its site is happycourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Happy Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page runs on Pegasus Hermes, the courier software behind
[Pack-Man](../pack-man/README.md)'s former voucher search, and shows vouchers, several separated by
commas, with their status, order number, the partner's voucher when another courier delivers and the
sender. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The voucher search opens numbers from its `r18p01` parameter.
