# Riders Express

Riders Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Riders Express is a postal operator and courier in Warsaw, Poland, with a city service by bike,
scooter and van, its own regional branches and partners for the rest of Poland, and international
networks abroad. Its site is riders.com.pl.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Riders Express number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page loads a shipment's status into the page from a script, with times from the
courier's handheld scanner. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page fills in the answer from a script and reads no number from its address, so no
address opens one item.
