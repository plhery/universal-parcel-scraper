Replies of the public Australian tracking service, `https://dragonflyshipping.com.au/cfworker/v3/tracking/<number>/`,
as the Wayback Machine captured them for the public samples in `numbers.json`:

- `delivered.json`: the first sample, from the later capture its note cites.
- `address-problem-then-delivered.json`, `after-failed-attempt.json` and `out-for-delivery.json`: the
  captures the other records cite.
- `not-found.json`: the service's reply to an invented number.

The suburb and state that older replies put on a round or a delivery are blanked: they are the recipient's.
