# Fixtures

Replies of 3CQ's `api/tracking/list` lookup for the two numbers of the indexed 17TRACK links in
[`numbers.json`](../numbers.json)
(<https://www.17track.net/zh-cn/carriers/3cq?nums=888002598569574> and
<https://www.17track.net/zh-cn/carriers/3cq?nums=888002507522830>).
`delivered-contagem.json` and `delivered-sao-paulo.json` go from 3CQ's warehouse in China through
Hong Kong, Doha and Brazil's customs to delivery by J&T Express Brazil. `not-found.json` is the
answer for a made-up number of the same shape.

The J&T couriers' names are replaced with `EXAMPLE COURIER`.
