# Fixtures

Replies of E-Commerce International's tracking page data, `ecommerce-system.com/track/{number}/__data.json`.

- `delivered.json`: a parcel registered in Poland, sent through the warehouse in Kyiv to Nova
  Poshta and collected at a pick-up point, with Nova Poshta's number.
- `not-found.json`: the reply for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers and times are invented.
