# SofEx Post

SofEx Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

SofEx Post, SofEx Post SRL of Chișinău, carries parcels from Israel to Moldova, Ukraine and Georgia
by sea or air, from its warehouse in Israel through customs to door-to-door delivery.

## Numbers

Its tracking box takes the code the courier gives the sender; the box's example has seven digits.
No public page shows a real number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking box, in a pop-up on the home page, posts the number to a handler on the site and shows
the parcel's stage: the warehouse in Israel, loading, shipping, customs, the distribution stock,
delivery and delivered. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracker takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one shipment.
