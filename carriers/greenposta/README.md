# Greenposta

Greenposta parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Greenposta, Greenposta sh.p.k. of Tirana, is an Albanian courier that presents itself as an
eco-friendly parcel delivery service. It delivers across Albania and to Kosovo and North
Macedonia.

## Numbers

Its tracker finds an order by its number. No public page shows a whole number or its layout, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page shows the order's status history, each step with who holds the parcel, when
it changed and any comments. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no
adapter.

## Links

`https://green-posta.al/Tracking?trackingNumber={trackingNumber}` opens the tracker on the
number, as its form sends it. A pasted `green-posta.al` tracking link names Greenposta.
