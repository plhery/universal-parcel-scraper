Replies of `POST http://www.yijiangky.com/query.aspx?pID=3&v=<random>` with the form the tracking page's
script sends, as received. `delivered.json` is the reply to the public sample in
[`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), and `not-found.json` the reply to the invented `YJ100200300400`. The
tests derive their other replies from these.
