Replies of Sendy's `GET /tracking/<number>?lang=en`. `SDS1703325.json` is the reply to the first
public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), with the recipient's name, phone, address and
position, the captain's name, phone and photo, the van's plate and position, and the image links
replaced. `not-found.json` is the HTTP 422 answer for a number Sendy holds nothing under. The tests
derive their other parcels from the sample with invented numbers.
