# BIZ Courier

BIZ Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

BIZ Courier & Logistics is a national courier in Koropi, Attica, Greece. Its site is bizcourier.eu.

## Numbers

No public page shows a BIZ Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its Track iT page takes up to 150 numbers and shows each one's status, local delivery station,
last-mile partner number and full history, with delivery attempts, calls, SMS and emails. With no
public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The Track iT page opens a number from its path, after the language, as in `/app/en/`.
