# TForce Integrated Solutions

TForce Integrated Solutions parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

TForce Integrated Solutions is TFI International's Canadian retail delivery company, formerly ATS Andlauer's
retail solutions division. TForce Logistics and TForce Freight are separate entries. Its site is
tforce-solutions.com.

## Numbers

Its numbers are nine digits, as two archived lookups on its own tracker show. Nine digits are shared by other
carriers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its quick trace posts the number to the site's WordPress endpoint without a session or CAPTCHA, but answers the
archived numbers and an unknown one alike with an empty reply, so a missing parcel cannot be told apart.

## Links

The quick tracking page opens numbers from its `tracking` parameter.
