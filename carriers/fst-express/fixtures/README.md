Replies of FST Express's `POST /tms-saas-oms/expose/traceQueryResult`. `34LRU5071871.json` is
the reply to the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json): a job from abroad that
cleared customs in Sydney and that Australia Post delivered. The sender's, recipient's and
customer's names, companies, addresses, phones, emails and postcodes are blanked; everything else
is as the tracker sent it. `not-found.json` is the answer for an invented job number FST has no
record of. The tests derive their other jobs from the sample with invented numbers.
