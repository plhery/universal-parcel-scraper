# Direct Courier

Direct Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Direct Courier Services is a courier in Piraeus, Greece. Its site is directcourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Direct Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its home page tracks a voucher through a form that posts it. With no public number to check its
replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
