# Arco Spedizioni

Arco Spedizioni parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Arco Spedizioni S.p.A., of Monza, is an Italian road haulier. It carries freight across Italy
through its own branch network and abroad, with logistics, dangerous goods and food-grade
transport for wine and oil. Its site is arcospedizioni.it.

## Numbers

A shipment is found by three parts: the year in four digits, the branch in three digits and an
eight-digit shipment number. A sender can also search by its own reference with its customer
code. No public page shows a number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The search box on its home page asks for the year, the branch and the number in three separate
fields, and sends them with an invisible browser check (reCAPTCHA). The results page refuses a
request without a passed check. Customers also track in an account area.

## Links

The search box posts its fields, so no address opens one parcel.
