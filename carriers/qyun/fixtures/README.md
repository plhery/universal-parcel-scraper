Replies of Qyun Express's Hualei tracker. `compensated.json` is the live answer for the public sample
from a tracking directory's Qyun page, re-encoded as UTF-8 with the consignee, shipper, signature,
fee, account and weight fields blanked. `no-track.json` and `page-unknown.html` are the live answers
for a number in a shape the tracker cannot hold.
