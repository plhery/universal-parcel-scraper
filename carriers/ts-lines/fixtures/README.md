`container-*.json` and `bill-*.json` are the replies of T.S. Lines' container and bill searches
for public samples in `numbers.json`, and for invented numbers it does not hold. `his-*.json`
are the replies of its history service for a container under a bill, named by the bill and the
container. `captcha-*.json` are captchas named by their code, their single-use tokens replaced.
The files are indented; the tests edit them for the cases no sample shows.
