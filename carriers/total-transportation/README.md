# Total Transportation & Distribution

Total Transportation & Distribution parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Total Transportation & Distribution, or TTD, is a less-than-truckload and truckload carrier in
Rancho Cucamonga, California. It serves California, Arizona and Nevada and runs to Toronto,
Montreal and Calgary. Its tracker carries the name Total Transportation. Its site is ttdwest.com.

## Numbers

The only public example is CJN and nine digits, from an aggregator, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker is hosted by Carrier Logistics, whose open XML trace
[Dohrn Transfer](../dohrn-transfer/README.md)'s adapter reads.
The only known number is an aggregator's example.

## Links

The tracker's shipment page opens a PRO number from its `wpro1` parameter, and its entry page
from `pronum`.
