Synthetic replies of JRT's Hualei tracker: `track-dhl.json` is the interface's record for a parcel
found by the shipper's reference, with JRT's flight and clearance rows and the rows copied from
DHL after the hand-off; `track-ups.json` is a parcel on the line into the United States,
with JRT's flight rows and the rows copied from UPS in Chinese; `page-registered.html` is the tracking page for an order without scans
found by the shipper's reference, showing the platform's own row; `no-track.json` is the
interface's answer for it and for an unknown number, and `page-unknown.html` the page's answer
for an unknown number. Numbers, references, parties and clocks are invented, and so are the places, except the public
hub cities of `track-ups.json`, whose states set UPS's clocks.
