# Corlan Post Express

Corlan Post Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Corlan Post Express is the parcel service of Corlan Trans, a road haulier in Timiș county, Romania,
with routes from Romania to other European countries. Its site is corlanpostexpress.ro.

## Numbers

Its tracking form gives CPE followed by nine digits as an example. No public page shows a real
number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows a parcel's status and scans by its number. With no public number to check
its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `tr` parameter.
