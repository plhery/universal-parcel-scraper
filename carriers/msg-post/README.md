# MSG Post & Koeriers

MSG Post & Koeriers parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

MSG Post & Koeriers is a postal and parcel company in the Netherlands that delivers letters, parcels
and courier rides by bike, scooter and van from its own locations, with partners covering the rest
of the country. Most of its staff are people at a distance from the labour market. Its site is
msg.eu.

## Numbers

No public page shows an MSG Post & Koeriers number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page is on Veloyd, the delivery software MSG runs on, which many other carriers share.
The page asks for one shipment over a websocket and shows its delivery or pickup date, a time
window, the stage it has reached and any delivery requirements, not a scan history. With no public
number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens a number from its path. Other carriers on Veloyd share its address, so a
link there does not name MSG.
