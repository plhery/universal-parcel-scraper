# F2 Logistics

F2 Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

F2 Logistics is the brand of F2 Logistics Philippines, Inc. in Parañaque, Metro Manila. It moves
domestic air, sea and land freight with door-to-door delivery across Luzon, the Visayas and
Mindanao, and also runs cold chain, warehousing and customs brokerage. Its site is
f2logistics.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes PWB and HWB references. No public page shows one and their format is unknown,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page frames tnt.f2logistics.com, which first asks for consent under the Data Privacy
Act and then posts the reference through a form. The home page still links an older tracker that
answers only over plain HTTP, which the catalog does not use.

## Links

The tracker takes the reference in a POST form after its consent screen, so no address opens one
shipment.
