# Argos S.A.

Argos S.A. parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Argos S.A. (ΑΡΓΟΣ Α.Ε.) is a press distributor in Koropi, Attica, Greece, that also carries parcels
and freight for businesses and consumers under the title ACT. Its site is argoscom.gr.

## Numbers

Its tracking form accepts 13 digits, nine digits starting with 80, or two letters, nine digits and
GR. No public page shows a whole number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page finds a waybill by its barcode. With no public number to check its replies
against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
