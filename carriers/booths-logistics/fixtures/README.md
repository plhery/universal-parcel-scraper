Replies of Booth's `ConsignmentTrack` service on `portal.booths.co.nz`. `search-BCPTEST8.json`
and `consignment-BCPTEST8.json` answer the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json):
its search and its consignment, with the job's pickup and delivery addresses and coordinates
blanked. `search-not-found.json` is the search for a number Booth's has no consignment under.
The tests derive their other consignments from the sample with invented numbers.
