# Crosswin

Crosswin parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Crosswin, formerly Romania Express, is a cross-border e-commerce consolidator in Tychy, Poland. It
carries parcels to Romania, Hungary, Bulgaria and other countries of central and south-eastern
Europe and hands them to local carriers and eMAG easybox lockers there. Its site is crosswin.pl.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Crosswin number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows each parcel of a shipment with its status history, from the label to
delivery attempts and the locker pickup deadline. With no public number to check its replies
against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens a number from its path, as in `/tracking/`.
