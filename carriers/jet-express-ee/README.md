# Jet Express

Jet Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Jet Express, Jet Express OÜ of Tallinn, is an Estonian courier and transport company: same-day
courier in Tallinn, deliveries across Estonia, and international road, air and sea freight. Its
tracker is a white-label Opter Track & Trace page, the platform [Cargobus](../cargobus/README.md)
uses too.

## Numbers

Its tracker finds a shipment by its order number. No public page shows a whole number or its
layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

With no public number to check the tracker's replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

`https://portal.kuller.ee/opter/TT?keyword={trackingNumber}` opens the tracker on the number.
