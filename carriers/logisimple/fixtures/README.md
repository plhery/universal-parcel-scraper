# Fixtures

Tracking pages of Logisimple's customer portal, `portal.logisimple.pt/pt/tracking?tracking={number}`,
cut down to the search form and the shipment's card.

- `delivered.html`: a shipment taken to two warehouses, booked with the recipient and
  delivered, with the proof of delivery the delivered scan opens.
- `not-found.html`: the page for a number the portal does not know.

Numbers, places, warehouse codes and contact details, the sender's reference, the recipient
and the proof of delivery's image are invented, and the dates are moved.
