Replies of Coretrails' `GET /api/tracking?tracking_number=`. `delivered-CRT330596304272.json` is
the whole reply to the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json). `not-found.http` is the
status line and headers of the answer for a number Coretrails has no shipment under, whose body is
empty. The tests derive their other shipments from the sample with invented numbers.
