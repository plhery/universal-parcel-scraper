# Plycon Transportation Group

Plycon Transportation Group parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Plycon Transportation Group (Plycon Van Lines, Inc.) is a New York final-mile carrier for
furniture and other large items, with terminals across the United States. Its sites are
plycongroup.com and plyconvanlines.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker asks for 21 digits, a length other carriers' numbers share, and no public number
has been found, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker on track.plyconvanlines.com answers a plain address without a session, but no
public number has been found to check a found reply with.

## Links

The tracker opens a number from the `trackingnum` parameter of `singletrack.php`.
