Replies of Copa Airlines Cargo's SmartKargo tracker, opened from its `AWBPrefix` and `AWBNo` query, with
scripts, styles and the ASP.NET form state removed.

- `delivered.html`: the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), a shipment from Guayaquil
  through Panama to New York. Its waybill list says it was delivered at JFK while the shipment panel
  still shows the arrival at Panama.
- `not-found.html`: the answer for an invented waybill with a valid check digit.
