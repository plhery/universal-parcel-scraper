# Quriers

Quriers parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Quriers is a courier in Łódź, Poland, with partner branches in Katowice, Kraków, Warsaw and Wrocław,
carrying parcels, cargo and letters across Poland and abroad. Some national parcels travel on DPD,
DHL or InPost waybills, which those networks track. Its site is quriers.pl.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Quriers number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows a waybill's status history. With no public number to check its replies
against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
