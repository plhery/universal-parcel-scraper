Replies of Iberomail's `GET TrackAndTrace/search.php` for the public samples in
[`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), as the service sent them. `W313153.json` is a domestic delivery, with
the signatory's name in its delivery row replaced. `W299199.json` is a parcel sent abroad under a
partner's S10 number and sent back. `W296517.json` is a record whose rows carry no clock.
`not-found.json` is the answer for a number Iberomail has no shipment under. The tests derive their other
replies from these with invented numbers.
