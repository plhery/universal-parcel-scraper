# Alphastline

Alphastline parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter: its
registration agreement forbids scraping platform data and copying the site's content
commercially without written permission.

Alphastline (傲飞国际快线, Alphast) is a Chinese cross-border express line run by DSers, the
dropshipping platform. Its site is alphastline.com and its contact address is on dserspro.com.
17TRACK names it Alphastline and gives its scans on Beijing time.

## Numbers

No public source shows a whole Alphastline number. A parcel the app tracked was numbered `AF`
and 21 digits, and 17TRACK reported it from Alphastline. AF reads as the initials of 傲飞.
With that one parcel, no public number and no tracker to confirm it, the rule offering Alphastline
for that layout is low.

## Links
