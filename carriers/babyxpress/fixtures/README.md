Replies of Delyva's `POST /v1.0/order/track` for BabyXpress's company id. `consignment-*.json` are
the replies to the public samples in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), with the staff's phone, the
map's coordinates, the sender's account and the order's internal ids left out. `not-found.json` is
the answer for an invented number. The tests derive their other consignments from the samples.
