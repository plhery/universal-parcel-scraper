Both files are synthetic, modelled on the replies of CMT Express's Hualei track interface:
`track.json` holds an order on the line to Europe with the platform's order row, the shipper's
order and the Shenzhen warehouse's receipt, and `unknown.json` is the answer for a number the
tracker does not know. The interface's replies are GBK; the files are UTF-8. Numbers, ids,
references and clocks are invented, and the parties are left empty.
