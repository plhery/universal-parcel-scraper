# Gordom Logistika

Gordom Logistika parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Gordom Logistika is a parcel courier in Zagreb, Croatia, with cash on delivery, same-day and
Saturday delivery and lanes to and from Slovenia. It announces the delivery slot by SMS. Its site is
gordomlogistika.hr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Gordom Logistika number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page finds a shipment by its Gordom number. With no public number to check its replies
against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
