# West Postal Services

West Postal Services parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

West Postal Services (WEST A.E.) is a postal operator in Greece that delivers personal mail with a
barcode scan as proof of delivery, and runs a next-day and same-day courier. Its site is wps.com.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a West Postal Services number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page posts one or more numbers, separated by spaces, from a script and shows their
status in the page. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
