# Fixtures

Pages of Timely Titan's tracker, `tracking.html?number=`.

- `delivered.html`: a parcel from the shop's label through the overseas warehouse in Hong Kong
  and a forwarding line to the delivering post in China, forwarded under a postal number and
  delivered. The shop's row has a later clock than the row above it, as on the live page.
- `not-found.html`: the page for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers, the shop, people, phone numbers, offices, places and dates are invented.
