# Fixtures

Replies of Tiui's guide service, `/api/guia/<folio>`.

- `delivered.json`: the reply for the public sample in `numbers.json`, from the pickup's
  registration to its delivery. The sender, recipient, merchant, product, delivery photos and
  internal ids are replaced by synthetic ones; the movements' codes, wording and times are kept.
- `not-found.json`: the reply for a folio Tiui does not know.
