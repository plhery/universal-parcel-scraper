# ZipasGo

ZipasGo parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ZipasGo, run by Zipas, UAB of Vilnius and formerly called Parcel Stars, collects, sorts and
carries parcels from its centres in the Baltics and elsewhere in Europe, and hands them to partner
carriers for the last leg. Its tracking number is its own, and its page names the partner.

## Numbers

Its tracking form's example, two letters and ten digits, is a placeholder in the layout of Amazon's
German numbers. No public page shows a real number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads the number from its address and loads the parcel's events from the
site's own service. With no number known to be live, its replies cannot be checked, so there is
no adapter.

## Links

`https://zipasgo.com/lt/siuntimas?tid={trackingNumber}` opens the tracking page on the number.
