Replies of Huahai's Hualei tracker. `delivered-usps.json` and `delivered-uniuni.json` are the live
answers for two public samples from a tracking directory's Huahai page, re-encoded as UTF-8 with
the account and signatory fields blanked and the shipper's reference zeroed. `no-track.json` and
`page-unknown.html` are the live answers for `ZZQX00000000000`, a string in no shape the tracker
issues.
