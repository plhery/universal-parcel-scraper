Replies of GTI's Hualei tracker. `track.json` is the interface's record for the public sample
in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), with the shipper's account code replaced: the forwarder's
own rows from Putian, the platform's scripted rows from the airport to customs, and the rows
copied from USPS, with their doubles and the copies that lost their spaces. `no-track.json` and
`page-unknown.html` are the live answers for an unknown number.
