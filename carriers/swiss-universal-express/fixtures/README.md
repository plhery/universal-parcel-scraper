Replies of `GET https://swiss-universal-express.ch/tracking/<number>?lang=en`, without the
advert, footer and sign-in popup after the result, and with an invented form token.
`SUE8910020752265FR.html` is the reply to the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json),
and `not-found.html` the answer for an invented number far beyond the range in use. The tests
derive their other pages from these.
