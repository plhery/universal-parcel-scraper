Pages of `GET https://the-tracking.com/<number>/`, without the social buttons, counters and
analytics after the parcel's box. `PE912472376US.html`, `PE259451929CA.html`,
`PE967067300US.html` and `PE924935636US.html` are the replies to public samples in
[`numbers.json`](../numbers.json); in `PE967067300US.html` the name of whoever took the parcel is
replaced. `not-found.html` is the first answer for an invented number and `not-found-again.html`
a later one. The tests derive their other pages from the first sample with invented numbers and
rows.
