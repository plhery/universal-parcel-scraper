# Fixtures

Answers of `POST /ols/trace2/standard.do2?method:search`, kept in the shape the tracker serves
them. `in-transit.html` is the Belarusian portal's answer for a public Belarusian number on
its way to Minsk, and `redirected.html` the Kazakh portal's for a public Russian number handed
on to its return. `delivered.html` is synthetic, in the shape of a delivered answer, with an
invented number, client order, town and masked recipient. `not-found.html` is the notice for an
invented number.
