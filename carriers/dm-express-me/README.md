# DM Express

DM Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

DM Express, registered as DM EXPRESS DELIVERY d.o.o., is a Montenegrin courier for express
deliveries across Montenegro.

## Numbers

Its tracker finds a shipment by its number. No public page shows a whole number, so there is no
rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker shows the shipment's status timeline, its pickup and delivery dates and the
delivery vehicle's last known position. With no public number to check its replies against,
there is no adapter.

## Links

`https://track.dmexpress.me/track?code={trackingNumber}` opens the tracker on the number. A
pasted `track.dmexpress.me` link with a `code` names DM Express.
