Replies of Spedire's `POST /api/public/tracking`. `reference-29112537098.json` is the reply to the
public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), with the sender's and receiver's names, cities
and postcodes and the pickup booking replaced. `delivered.json` is synthetic: its numbers, places
and clocks are invented. `not-found.json` is the answer for a code neither platform has.
