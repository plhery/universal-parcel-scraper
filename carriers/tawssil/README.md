# Tawssil

Tawssil parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Tawssil is the parcel service of Cash Plus, the Moroccan money-transfer network. It delivers online shops' and
people's parcels across Morocco, to the door or to Cash Plus agencies, with cash on delivery. Its site is
tawssil.ma.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Tawssil number, and its merchant API lets senders choose the barcode, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker at tracking.tawssil.ma posts the barcode in a form and refuses visitors from outside Morocco.

## Links

Its tracker has no address for a single number, so no link names it.
