Replies of the GetInformationbyTracking action behind Islandwide's tracking page.

`delivered-002836100CABXND0000000.json` answers the public sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json),
with the delivery address, the signer's name, the signature, the scans' coordinates and addresses,
and the driver's and user's ids emptied, and the logo left out. `not-found.json` answers an invented
number. `version-changed.json` is the action's answer to a request naming an older build of the app.
