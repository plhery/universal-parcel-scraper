# Fixtures

Pages the consignment summary rendered in a browser after Cloudflare's check, for the public
samples in `numbers.json`. Each keeps the `window.consignment` script and the summary the page
drew from it; the portal's menus, styles, form state and the CDN's scripts are left out, and the
signature images are cut to their first characters.

Each sample's page is named after it: one delivered in Melbourne after a linehaul from Brisbane,
and one from Melbourne's pickup to Brisbane's delivery truck, without a delivery scan.
`not-found.html` is the page for an impossible number.
