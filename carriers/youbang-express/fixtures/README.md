Replies of YouBang Express's Hualei tracker. `track.json` is synthetic, in the shape of the
interface's record: the warehouse's two rows and the platform's scripted rows (`CO_C_CR`) in the
wordings the tracker writes, with an invented number, reference, account, product, destination
and clocks. `no-track.json` and `page-unknown.html` are the live answers for an invented number.
