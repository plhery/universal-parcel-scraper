# Fixtures

The pages follow the tracker's layout, cut down to the parts the parser reads, with yearless
rows newest first. `delivered.html` goes from the order to a signed delivery: a failed attempt
comes before it, one route plan is repeated, the booked day is in a row, and the delivering
depot is named once and called "Local Hub" after. `in-transit.html` waits at the depot for its
booked day across the turn of the year, after customer service updated the contact details.
`collection.html` is a collection from the customer, sent on to the retailer's returns site.
`crew.html` is the delivery-day page with the crew's first names, the stops before the
customer's, the planned window, the crew's current estimate and a map of the van. `not-found.html` is the form's "Invalid UPI
number" notice for a reference and postcode the tracker does not show.

References, postcodes, names, places, coordinates, keys and clocks are invented.
