# Transpress

Transpress parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Transpress, Транспрес ООД of Bozhurishte, is a Bulgarian carrier of pallets, parcels and freight
across the country and, as Dachser's partner, to the rest of Europe. Its sister company Транспрес
Деливъри ЕООД carries courier items under its own terms on the same site and tracker.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Transpress number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page finds a shipment by its waybill number. With no public number to check its replies
against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page's own form sends the number as `track-number` on the Bulgarian or English page,
so `transpress.bg/bg/prosledjavane?track-number={number}` opens one shipment, and a pasted link of
either language names Transpress.
