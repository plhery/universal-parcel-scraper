# Xuchuan International Logistics

Xuchuan International Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated
adapter.

Xuchuan International Logistics (旭川国际物流) is a Xiamen forwarder whose site is xcwl.ltd.
17TRACK names it XC, and relays China Post's labels on its line.

## Numbers

No public source shows a whole Xuchuan number. A parcel a universal provider files under XC has
`XC`, ten digits and `CN`; the corpus holds a made-up number in that shape. Two letters are a
thin mark of the company, so the rule is low.

## Why there is no adapter

The site's tracking box hands the number to 17TRACK's widget, and the company runs no tracker of
its own.
