# Inter Express Logistics

Inter Express Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Inter Express Logistics (IEL) is a Thai parcel and freight carrier with chilled and frozen
services. Its site is iel.co.th.

## Numbers

Archived lookups on its own systems show three letters, the last digit of the year, the month
and day, and a five-digit sequence. `IEL` is its own prefix; other prefixes appear to be
customers' codes. `inter-express-logistics-1` suggests IEL at low confidence for its own prefix,
from one number; other prefixes have no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker answers a plain address without a session, but the archived numbers are from 2020
and no longer answered, so no current reply has been checked.

## Links

The tracker opens a number from its `trackingNo` parameter.
