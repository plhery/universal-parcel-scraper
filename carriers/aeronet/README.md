# Aeronet Worldwide

Aeronet Worldwide parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Aeronet Worldwide, Inc., of Irvine, California, forwards expedited, aircraft-on-ground, domestic
and international freight from stations across the US, with customs brokerage and warehousing.
It issues its own housebills. Its site is aeronet.com.

## Numbers

Shipments are found by housebill or by reference number. Aeronet publishes no format and no
public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page posts the housebill or reference through the site's WordPress form handler and
shows the answer in the page, with no address per number. No public number is known to see a
found reply. Customers also track in the eSolutions account portal.

## Links

The tracking page posts its form, so no address opens one parcel.
