# BCS Courier

BCS Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

B.C.S. Courier is a courier in Athens, Greece. Its site is bcscourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a BCS Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page runs on Pegasus Hermes, the courier software behind
[Pack-Man](../pack-man/README.md)'s former voucher search, and shows a voucher's status, the
partner's voucher when another courier delivers, the sender, the recipient, and the pickup and
delivery dates. It finds a shipment by its voucher or its notice number. With no public number to
check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The voucher search opens numbers from its `r18p01` parameter.
