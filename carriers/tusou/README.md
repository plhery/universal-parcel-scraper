# Tusou

Tusou parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Tusou is a small Chinese cross-border logistics company selling postal, express and FBA lines. Its site is
tusoukeji.cn, and its tracker is its tenant on the Kingtrans forwarder platform.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Tusou number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker reads an open XML lookup without a session or CAPTCHA, on the platform TakeSend uses, and
answers an unknown number with no rows. No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

The tracker's results page takes numbers from its `bills` parameter.
