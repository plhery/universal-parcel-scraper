`bl-*.json`, `booking-*.json` and `container-*.json` are the replies of the B/L, booking and
container screens of Wan Hai's app for public samples in `numbers.json` and their containers.
`movement-*.json` are the replies of its movement screen for a sample's container, named by the
booking and the container. The files are indented; the tests edit them for the cases no sample
shows.
