Replies of Suoexpress's Hualei tracker. `delivered.json` is the live answer for the public sample
from a tracking directory's SUOCANG page, re-encoded as UTF-8 with the consignee, shipper,
signature, fee, account and weight fields blanked. `no-track.json` and `page-unknown.html` are the
live answers for `ZZ0000000000ZZ`, a string in no shape the tracker issues.
