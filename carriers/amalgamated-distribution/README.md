# Amalgamated Distribution

Amalgamated Distribution parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Amalgamated Distribution is a Melbourne courier and taxi-truck company delivering across Victoria. Its site is
amaldist.com.au.

## Numbers

Its prepaid sticker and consignment numbers are short runs of digits that other carriers print too, and no public
page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker reads the number from a form or its address without a session or CAPTCHA and answers an unknown
number with an empty table.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `item` parameter.
