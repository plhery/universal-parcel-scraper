# Fixtures

Replies of Xindus's `shipment-tracking/shipment` lookup for the two air waybills of the public
shipment report in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json)
(<https://www.scribd.com/document/894923335/Tracking-Details-Jul-28-2025-to-Jul-28-2025>).
`delivered-usps.json` and `delivered-uniuni.json` go from the booking in Jaipur through export
clearance in Delhi to delivery by USPS and by UniUni. `not-found.json` is the answer for a number
of the same shape that Xindus holds no shipment for.

The staff accounts that wrote Xindus's own scans, and the shipments' postcodes, are removed.
