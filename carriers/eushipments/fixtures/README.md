# Fixtures

Replies of euShipments' tracking form handler, `admin-ajax.php?action=track_order_form`.

- `delivered.html`: the piece of HTML for an order taken from the warehouse through two hubs to a
  Bulgarian courier and delivered, with a row the page lists twice and the courier's name,
  number and tracking link after the table.
- `not-found.json`: the error for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers, places, hubs, dates and the courier's wordings are invented.
