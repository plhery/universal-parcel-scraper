# DK Logistics

DK Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

DK Logistics, DK LOGISTICS OÜ of Tallinn, is an Estonian international courier and forwarder for
documents and small parcels, with air and road freight and customs clearance.

## Numbers

Its tracking page finds a shipment by its waybill (HAWB) number or by a reference. No public page
shows a whole number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form posts the number with its kind to a handler on the site. With no public number to
check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracker takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one shipment.
