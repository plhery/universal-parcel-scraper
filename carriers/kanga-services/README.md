# Kanga Services

Kanga Services parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Kanga Services is a family courier in Athens, Greece, with a service for biological samples and
international freight. Its site is kanga.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Kanga Services number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page embeds a form from Pegasus Hermes, the courier software behind
[Pack-Man](../pack-man/README.md)'s former voucher search, that posts vouchers, several separated by
commas. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
