# Fixtures

Cut-down pages of Runner Express's tracker at `/track/?search_query={number}`, without the site's menus,
footer and scripts.

- `returned.html`: the page for the public sample in `numbers.json`, a parcel received at the Shenzhen
  warehouse, flown to Israel, cleared, left uncollected at a service point and returned to the shipper. Each
  scan states its offset. The page names no recipient.
- `not-found.html`: "This logistics item was not found", the tracker's answer for a synthetic number of the
  sample's shape.
