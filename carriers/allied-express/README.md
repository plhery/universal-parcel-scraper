# Allied Express

Allied Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Allied Express Transport is an Australian courier and express freight carrier, part of the
Freightways group, with its head office in Sydney. It runs couriers, taxi trucks and overnight
road express (Allied Overnight Express) across Australia. Its site is alliedexpress.com.au.

## Numbers

No public page shows an Allied Express consignment number, so there is no rule. Numbers that
shipping platforms print on Allied labels are the platforms' own references.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker is a form that posts the consignment or reference number with the destination
postcode to its iTrack service. A lookup without the postcode is refused.

## Links

No address opens one parcel. The site's terms allow links to its home page, which carries the
tracking form, so the catalog links there.
