# International Bridge

International Bridge parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

International Bridge, Inc. is a small-parcel shipper for online sellers in Provo, Utah. It ships
parcels to Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, Guam, the US Virgin Islands and other countries under its
own tracking numbers and hands the last mile to USPS and partner carriers. ParcelPool was its
domestic delivery brand. Its site is myib.com.

## Numbers

Its tracking numbers have no published layout, and no public page shows one, so there is no rule.
The tracker also takes merchants' order numbers, which need not be unique.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page reads `GET tracking.myib.com/api/tracking/events?reference=<number>`. No public
number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

`tracking.myib.com/?trackingnumber=<number>` opens one parcel.
