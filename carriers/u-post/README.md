# U Post

U Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

U Post is an international courier between Ukraine and the European Union, with its own warehouses
in Ukraine and Slovakia, a customs broker in Mukachevo and partners across the EU. It carries
documents, parcels and freight, and clears customs both ways. U Post International runs it from
Ukraine, and U POST s. r. o. is its Slovak postal company. Its sites are upost.com.ua and upost.sk.

## Numbers

No public page shows a U Post number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page, the same on both sites, finds a parcel by its U Post number. With no public
number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
