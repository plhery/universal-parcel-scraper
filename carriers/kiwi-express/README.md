# Kiwi Express

Kiwi Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Kiwi Express (Kiwi Oversize) is a New Zealand courier for oversize and heavy items, a Freightways
brand. Its sites are kiwiexpress.co.nz and kiwioversize.co.nz.

## Numbers

Its tracker says barcodes are `KO` and ten digits; consignment numbers can be free text. No
public barcode has been found, so there is no rule yet.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker on my.kiwiexpress.co.nz answers a plain address without a session, but no public
barcode has been found to check a found reply with.

## Links

The tracker's address carries the barcode in its path.
