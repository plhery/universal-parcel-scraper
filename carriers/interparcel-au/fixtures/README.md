# Fixtures

Tracking pages the browser rendered after Cloudflare's check, for the public samples in
`numbers.json`, cut down to the tracking pane as in
[Interparcel UK's fixtures](../../interparcel-uk/fixtures/README.md).

Each sample's page is named after it: a Couriers Please parcel from Melbourne to Sydney, and a
StarTrack parcel from New South Wales to Victoria whose detailed list repeats a scan. `not-found.html` is the empty
track form the site answered for an impossible reference.
