# MVP Courier

MVP Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

MVP Courier is a courier in Athens, Greece, for postal items and temperature-controlled medicines.
Its site is mvpcourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows an MVP Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page, a white label of the Synlog platform, shows each status change, from created,
loaded and out for delivery to delivered with or without a signature, not delivered or cancelled, in
UTC. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `barcode` parameter.
