Replies of Next Smart Ship's `POST /oms/v3/tracks/v2/query`. `NSS3623110762978891776.json` and
`NSS3309831094921615360.json` are the whole replies to the public samples in
[`numbers.json`](../numbers.json): a parcel from China delivered by USPS and one within Australia
delivered by Australia Post. `not-found.json` is the answer for an invented number Next Smart
Ship has no record of. The tests derive their other entries from the samples with invented
numbers.
