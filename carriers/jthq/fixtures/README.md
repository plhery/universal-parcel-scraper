# Fixtures

- `in-transit.txt`: the lookup's reply for a public sample from a tracking directory's JTHQ page,
  as received: a parcel to Canada, landed at Toronto, with Canada Post's number.
- `canada-post.txt`: the reply for another sample from that page, as received: Canada Post's scans
  copied after clearance, out for delivery.
- `usps.txt`: the reply for an earlier sample from that page, as received: the line to the United
  States, delivered by USPS.
- `not-found.txt`: the reply for the invented `JTHQ000000000000`.
