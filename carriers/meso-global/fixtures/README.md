Synthetic replies of Meso-global's Hualei tracker: `track.json` has an order to Germany whose rows
carry the wordings a universal provider relays from the tracker, each written at its own clock.
`no-track.json` is the interface's answer for an unknown number, and `page-unknown.html` the
tracking page's, without its scripts and styles. Numbers, references, parties and clocks are
invented.
