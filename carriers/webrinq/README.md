# WebrinQ

WebrinQ parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

WebrinQ is a last-mile parcel carrier for online shops in Denmark, with same-day and evening home
delivery from its own terminals, parcel shops and CityHub lockers. Its site is webrinq.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows a WebrinQ number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page finds a parcel by the shop's tracking number or the parcel number under the label
barcode, and shows its status and history with only the recipient's postcode and city. The full
address needs the recipient's phone number. With no public number to check its replies against,
there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens a number from its path, or from its `q` parameter.
