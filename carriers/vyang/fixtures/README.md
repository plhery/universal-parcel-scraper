Replies of VYANG's Hualei tracker. `delivered.json` is the live answer for the public sample from a
tracking directory's VYANG page, re-encoded as UTF-8 with the consignee, shipper, signature, fee,
account and weight fields blanked. `no-track.json`, the record that only echoes the number, and
`page-unknown.html` are the live answers for an invented number.
