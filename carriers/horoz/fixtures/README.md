# Fixtures

Cut-down delivery notes from Horoz Lojistik's tracker at `frmAtfDetay.aspx?Ptn={number}`: the
headline, the note's details, the masked sender and recipient, the products and the movements,
oldest first.

- `delivered.html`: a parcel carried through a transfer centre and delivered, with a row the
  page lists twice and the delivery as the last movement.
- `not-found.html`: the page for a number the tracker does not know, with every field empty and
  no movement table.

Numbers, masked names, places, centres, plates and dates are invented.
