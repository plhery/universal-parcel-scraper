# ACE Logistics Estonia

ACE Logistics Estonia parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ACE Logistics Estonia is the Estonian office of ACE Logistics, a Baltic road freight and parcel group. Its
tracker is shared with ACE Logistics Lithuania and Latvia on acelogistics.eu.

## Numbers

No public page shows an ACE Logistics Estonia number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker renders results as HTML without a session or CAPTCHA and answers an unknown reference with a
no-data message. No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

The shared tracker opens references from its `ref` parameter; a link names Estonia's office with `office=EE`.
