# PostModern

PostModern parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

PostModern is the postal brand of MEDIA Logistik GmbH in Dresden. It delivers letters, registered
mail and parcels itself in eastern Saxony around Dresden and in Chemnitz and southwest Saxony, and
through partners across Germany. Its site is post-modern.de.

## Numbers

No public page shows a PostModern number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page posts the number from a form to paketversand.post-modern.de, which tracks
parcels and registered mail. Its imprint reserves text and data mining under § 44b UrhG. No public
number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

The tracker takes the number only through its form, so no address opens one parcel.
