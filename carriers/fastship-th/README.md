# FastShip

FastShip parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

FastShip is the brand of FastShip Co., Ltd. in Bangkok. It ships Thai sellers' parcels abroad as
their shipping agent: it takes drop-offs at Thailand Post branches and hands parcels to UPS,
FedEx, Aramex, SF Express, USPS, PostNL or Thailand Post. Its site is fastship.co.

## Numbers

Its tracker's placeholder shows FS, seven digits and TH. No public page shows a FastShip number,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page at `track.fastship.co` renders one parcel on the server, with its status steps
and the partner carrier's number. No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

`track.fastship.co/<number>` opens one parcel.
