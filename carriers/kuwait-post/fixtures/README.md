`forwarded.json` is the browser helper's reading of the tracker's reply for the item a review on [TrackingMore's Kuwait Post page](https://www.trackingmore.com/review-kuwait-post.html) quotes. The helper keeps each scan's country, event, wall clock, office and non-delivery details, and drops the office's local id, the next office, the mail category, the item's condition and the signatory.
