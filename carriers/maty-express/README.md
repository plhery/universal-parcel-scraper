# Maty Express

Maty Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Maty Express carries parcels between Spain, Belgium and Romania for Romanians abroad, with pickup and
delivery at home. Its site is matyexpress.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Maty Express number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page takes an AWB through a form and shows the parcel's route, packages and delivery.
With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
