# Maraton

Maraton parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Maraton is a courier in Poznań, Poland, delivering within the city by bike, motorbike and car,
sending parcels by train conductors, and carrying national and foreign parcels through partners. Its
site is maraton.poznan.pl.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Maraton number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page finds a parcel by its Maraton waybill number. With no public number to check its
replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
