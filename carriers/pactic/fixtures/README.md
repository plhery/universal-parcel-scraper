Synthetic replies of Pactic's tracking lookup, shaped as its live replies: `delivered.json` is
the bare list it returns for a parcel of its own from the order to the delivery at a parcel
locker, newest first, `envelope.json` the `{status, data, error}` envelope an archived reply
had, with the statuses out of order, and `not-found.json` the empty list for a waybill it does
not know. Waybills and clocks are invented.
