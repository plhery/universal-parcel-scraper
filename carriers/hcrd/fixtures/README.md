Replies of HCRD's Hualei tracker. `delivered.json` and `in-transit.json` are the live answers for
two public samples from a tracking directory's HCRD page, re-encoded as UTF-8 with the consignee,
shipper, signature, fee, account and weight fields blanked. `no-track.json` and `page-unknown.html`
are the live answers for an invented number.
