Pages of Planète Courrier's tracker, cut to the part between its search box and its footer. The
`detail-*` files are order pages (`?r=<n>&detail=true`) and the `list-*` files the list a number
names (`?r=<n>`), for the public samples in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json): a delivered order,
an order delivered after a failed attempt, and an older order handed to FedEx, which the list now
calls expired. The recipient's address and signature sit behind the page's postcode check and are
not in them. `detail-unknown.html` and `list-unknown.html` are the replies for an invented number.
