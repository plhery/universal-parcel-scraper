# LVZ Post

LVZ Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

LVZ Post, LVZ Post GmbH of Leipzig, is the private postal company of the media group that publishes
the Leipziger Volkszeitung. It carries letters, registered mail and parcels in Leipzig and its
region, and onward across Germany. Its site is lvz-post.de.

## Numbers

The site's search box hands its tracker anything it takes for a number: DD, CH, E, BZ, P or N14
followed by digits, or five digits or more. That matches far too many numbers for a rule, and no
public page shows a whole number.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page posts the number from a form to its parcel portal at paketversand.lvz-post.de,
which tracks registered mail and parcels. The portal runs on Hybrilog's parcel portal software, as
those of [NordBrief](../nordbrief/README.md), [MZZ-Briefdienst](../mzz-briefdienst/README.md) and
[PostModern](../post-modern/README.md) do. No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

The portal takes the number only through its form, so no address opens one parcel.
