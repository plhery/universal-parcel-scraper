# Fixtures

Whole replies of `Frm_DocTrackWeb.aspx`, kept as served, for a public sample in `numbers.json` and for a made-up
number. Each shows the number echoed, the booking detail (from and to centers, day and clock, consignee) and the
status line; the transit and delivery tables follow, oldest first.

- `delivered.html`: an air waybill booked in Pune, scanned in and out of two regional offices, received at its
  delivering hub and delivered. Each office name is a link whose tooltip holds the office's address and phone
  numbers.
- `not-found.html`: the reply for a made-up number: the number echoed, every field empty, the not-found status
  and both tables with their headers alone.
