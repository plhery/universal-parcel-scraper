Replies of Expeditors' guest tracking service for two public samples whose numbers and sources are in
`numbers.json`. `shipment-{number}.json` is the guest search's answer, which is also the shipment's
`/details` section, and `events-{number}.json` its `/events` section. The customer reference is invented; the
replies carry no parties, and every event's remarks were empty. `not-found.json` is the search's answer for an
invented reference.
