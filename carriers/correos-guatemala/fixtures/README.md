`delivered.html`, `returning.html` and `customs.html` are the tracker's replies for three items listed in the international claims reports Correos de Guatemala publishes under its access-to-information law, [October 2026](https://correos.gob.gt/LAIP-2021/articulo-10/inciso-29/2026/octubre/art%2010%20inciso%2029%20internacional%20octubre%202026.pdf) and [January 2025](https://correos.gob.gt/LAIP-2021/articulo-10/inciso-29/2025/enero/art%2010%20inciso%2029%20internacional%20enero%202025.pdf). They are trimmed of the site's navigation, contact column and footer; the full history comment is kept as served. `not-found.html` is the reply for a synthetic item code.
