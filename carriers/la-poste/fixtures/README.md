# La Poste fixtures

- `delivered.json`: `suivi-unifie` response for a delivered Colissimo parcel, with `group`/`code` pairs, a completed timeline step, a delivery estimate and the recipient blocks the feed carries. All identifiers, names, addresses and times are synthetic.
- `locator.html`: the start of a `localiser.laposte.fr` point page, with the point's record in `Yext["profile"]`: its address, contact and opening hours. All names, numbers and addresses are synthetic.
