# Home Delivery Team

Home Delivery Team parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Home Delivery Team (HDT), run by GE Logisztika Kft., delivers online shops' orders across
Hungary, from parcels to appliances and furniture that its two-person crews carry in. Its site
is homedt.hu.

## Numbers

Its tracking page asks for the order number, the last group of digits on the waybill. Digits
alone match many other carriers' numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows an order's scans by its order number. With no public number to check
its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `track_number` parameter.
