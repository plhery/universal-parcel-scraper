# DK Express Courier

DK Express Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

DK Express Courier is a courier for online shops and businesses near Piraeus, Greece. It delivers in
Attica with its own vehicles, and across Greece, to Cyprus and abroad through ACS. Its site is
dkexpresscourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a DK Express Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page takes up to 10 numbers and shows each one in five stages: Καταχωρήθηκε, Σε
μεταφορά, Σε διανομή, Παραδόθηκε, or Χρειάζεται προσοχή when a delivery is blocked. With no public
number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `voucher` parameter.
