# Trustexpress

Trustexpress parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Trustexpress, Тръстекспрес ООД of Sofia, is a Bulgarian courier for express and economy parcels,
delivered to addresses and through its own offices across the country.

## Numbers

Its tracking page finds a shipment by its waybill number. No public page shows a whole number or
its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form posts the number with a token from the page's session. With no public number to
check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracker takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one shipment.
