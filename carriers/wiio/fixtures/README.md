Synthetic replies of PD Express's Hualei tracker for Wiio: `track.json` has a delivered order
whose rows mix the platform's own, to the second on Beijing's clock, with the airline's and the
delivery carrier's, to the minute on their own clocks. `no-track.json` is the interface's answer
for an unknown number, and `page-unknown.html` the tracking page's, trimmed. Numbers, references,
parties and clocks are invented.
