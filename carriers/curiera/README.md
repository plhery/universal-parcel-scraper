# Curiera

Curiera parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Curiera delivers parcels in Bucharest and Ilfov with electric vehicles, and across Romania through
partners. Its site is curiera.ro.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Curiera number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page, hosted on CourierManager, a courier software platform, takes an AWB through a form and shows its scans. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

Its tracking page takes numbers only through a form that posts them, so no address opens one item.
