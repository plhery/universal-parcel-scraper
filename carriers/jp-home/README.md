# JP HOME

JP HOME parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter yet.

JP HOME is the Jacky Perrenot group's home delivery of bulky goods in France, Belgium and
Luxembourg. It took over Agediss, AB Home and VIR by JP. [Tamdis](../tamdis/README.md) and
[ArrowXL](../arrow-xl/README.md) keep their own entries.

## Numbers

Recipients quote eight- and nine-digit counters, lengths many carriers share, so no rule names
it.

## Tracker

The tracking form asks a JSON interface without a session or CAPTCHA, but every lookup needs the
delivery postcode beside the number.

## Links

Its tracking page reads the number from its `reference` parameter, and Agediss's former
tracker kept it in the path.
