# Tonami Transportation

Tonami Transportation parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Tonami Transportation (トナミ運輸) is a major Japanese less-than-truckload carrier based in Toyama.
Its site is tonami.co.jp.

## Numbers

Its inquiry numbers are ten digits with a mod-7 check digit, a layout other Japanese carriers'
numbers and several rules share, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker answers a plain address without a session and answered public numbers; an adapter is
possible and not yet written. It keeps about three months of history, and a parcel it hands to a
partner shows the partner's number.

## Links

The tracker opens a number from its `id[0]` parameter.
