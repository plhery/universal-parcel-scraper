# UBX UK

UBX UK parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

UBX UK (United Business Xpress) is a Slough courier for international and domestic express. Its site is
ubx.uk.net.

## Numbers

No public page shows a UBX UK number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker runs on Courier Navigator, as Hart2Hart Express's does, and reads an open JSONP endpoint without a
session or CAPTCHA, which answers an unknown number with an empty list. No public number is known to see a
found reply.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `consign_note` parameter, on ubx.uk.net and on its Courier Navigator host.
