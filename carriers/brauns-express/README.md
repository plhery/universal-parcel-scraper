# Braun's Express

Braun's Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Braun's Express, Inc., of Dalton, Georgia, is a regional less-than-truckload carrier that
specialises in floor covering. Its terminals serve the Northeast, the Mid-Atlantic and the
Midwest. Its site is braunsexpress.com.

## Numbers

Shipments are found by PRO number or by the shipper's bill of lading number. Braun's publishes no
format and no public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page takes the number in its address and answers HTML, with "No Records Found" for
an unknown number. No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

`www.braunsexpress.com/tracking/?tracking_number=<number>` opens one shipment.
