# Fixtures

Pages from Viapost's tracker for the public samples in `numbers.json`, with the page's styles left out
and the recipient's name, phone and street address blanked, in the recipient block and in the
Viber/SMS notices:

- `delivered.html`: `RR745733230HU`, an AliExpress parcel Magyar Posta handed over, with a failed
  delivery round before its delivery in Skopje.
- `tetovo.html`: `269793`, a web shop's order taken in at Skopje and delivered in Tetovo.
- `not-found.html`: the HTTP 500 page for the synthetic `99999999`, as received.
