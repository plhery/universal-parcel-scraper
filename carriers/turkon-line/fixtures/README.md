# Fixtures

Replies to the containers' lookup as the browser service passes them on, after
[its projection](../../../trawl/turkon-line-browser.mjs) drops weights, dangerous goods, reefer
settings, vessels and voyages.

- `booking-10186118.json`: the public sample's booking, whose bill is in `numbers.json`.
- `not-found.json`: the reply to a number Turkon does not hold.
- `synthetic-booking-two-containers.json`: synthetic. A booking far beyond the range Turkon
  issues, whose two containers' numbers fail their check digit, loaded on one vessel and due at
  the port of discharge.
