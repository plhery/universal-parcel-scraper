# ICC Courier

ICC Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ICC Courier (Intercity Courier) is a courier in Athens, Greece, with same-day and next-day delivery
in Attica, and delivery across Greece, to Cyprus and abroad. Its site is icccourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows an ICC Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page takes up to five numbers, separated by spaces, or an order number. It shows the
courier that carries each parcel, ICC or after a hand-off ACS Courier, ELTA Courier or Kronos
Express, with the piece count, pickup and delivery, checkpoints with their times, notes and the
receiver's name. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `voucher` parameter, in Greek or under `/en/`.
