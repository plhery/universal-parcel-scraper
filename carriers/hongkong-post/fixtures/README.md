`replies.json` holds synthetic chatbot replies: the status clock is invented and no reply names a
parcel. The wording, markup and envelopes follow the live chatbot's language, main and Mail
Tracing menus, its number prompt, and its found, no-record, invalid-number and out-of-sequence
answers.

`website-delivered.json` is the tracker's reply for the public sample `LP157079155HK` (source in
`numbers.json`). `website-not-found.json` is its reply for the synthetic, checksum-valid
`RR123456785HK`.
