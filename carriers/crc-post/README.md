# CRC Post

CRC Post items use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

CRC Post (CRC Post S.r.l., brand CRCPOST) is a private postal operator in Naples. It delivers
registered letters, judicial acts and traffic fines under a special licence, and parcels, through
its CRC Point branches in Campania. Its site is crcpost.it.

## Numbers

Its terms describe the barcode as letters and digits that identify one envelope. No public page
shows one or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form on its home page asks a Netlify function (`/.netlify/functions/tracking`, with
`barcode`) and shows one outcome with its dates, town and branch, not a history. No public number
is known to see a found reply.

## Links

The home page takes the barcode in its form, so no address opens one item.
