# City Courier

City Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

City Courier, formerly City Delivery, is a courier in Heraklion, Crete, Greece, with a partner
network in Crete, Athens and Thessaloniki. Its site is citycourier.gr.

## Numbers

No public page shows a City Courier number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page runs on Pegasus Hermes, the courier software behind
[Pack-Man](../pack-man/README.md)'s former voucher search, and shows up to 30 numbers at once, with
each one's status, order number, the partner's waybill when another courier delivers and the
estimated delivery date. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The voucher search opens numbers from its `r18p01` parameter.
