Replies of NuMark's `GET cgibin/wbprotrk`. `delivered.html` is the whole reply to the first public
sample in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json). `not-found.html` is the answer for an invented PRO
number far beyond the range in use, and `invalid.html` the answer for a number too long for the
trace. The tests derive their other pages from these.
