Replies of ShipAgent's `GET api.shipagent.in/api/v1/public/tracking/{number}?source=shipagent.in`.
`SA260899579915.json` and `59120810000103.json` answer the two public samples in
[`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), with the order's reference replaced by a placeholder; they
hold no name, phone or street. `not-found.json` is the answer for a number ShipAgent has no
shipment under. The tests derive their other shipments from the samples.
