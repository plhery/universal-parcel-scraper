# Fixtures

Pages from Daytona Express's tracker for public samples in `numbers.json`, whole.

- `pickup-to-delivery.html`: a pickup in Salvador through the São Paulo hub to its delivery in
  Joinville.
- `absent-recipient.html`: two absent recipients and a parcel held at the base before its
  delivery.
- `booked-delivery.html`: a booked delivery day and two delivery scans.
- `not-found.html`: the page for an invented protocol the tracker holds nothing for.
