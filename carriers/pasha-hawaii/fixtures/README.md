`container-*.html`, `bill-*.html` and `booking-*.html` are the tracker's replies to its
container, bill of lading and booking searches for public samples in `numbers.json`, named by
the number. `notfound-*.html` are its replies for invented numbers of each kind. Each reply keeps
its form and inquiry without the site's scripts, styles and links, with the party references
blanked. The tests edit them for the cases no sample shows.
