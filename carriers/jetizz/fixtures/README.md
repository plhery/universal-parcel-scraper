`returned.html` is the tracker's reply for the public sample Jetizz returned to its sender, and
`delivered.html` its reply for the partner's number of the one Aras Kargo delivered (sources in
`numbers.json`). `not-found.html` is its reply to an accepted picture for a number it does not
know, and `refused.html` its reply to a wrong answer.

`captcha-SEC6.jpg` and `captcha-HBJS.jpg` are pictures the tracker drew, named by the codes it
accepted for them.
