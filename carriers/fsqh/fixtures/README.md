Synthetic replies of Foshan Qianhe's Hualei track interface, in the shape of live ones, with
made-up numbers, ids and dates. `track.json` is a parcel on the line to the United States, which
GOFO delivers; `gb-line.json` one on the line to Britain, still in China, which Royal Mail
delivers. `unknown.json` is the interface's answer for a made-up number it does not know. The
interface's replies are GBK; the files are UTF-8.
