# Europoshta

Europoshta parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Europoshta (ЄВРОПОШТА) of Kyiv is a Ukrainian private postal operator: parcels up to 30 kg between
its branches and to addresses, and postal and money transfers and other financial services.

## Numbers

Its tracking page takes a shipment's number. No public page shows a whole number or its layout, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form sends the number to the tracking page in its address. With no public number to
check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

`https://europoshta.com.ua/tracking/?track_input={trackingNumber}&go_button=OK` opens the tracking
page on the number, as its form does. A pasted link to that page with a `track_input` number names
Europoshta.
