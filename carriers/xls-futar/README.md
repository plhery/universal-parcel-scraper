# XLS Futár

XLS Futár parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

XLS Futár Zrt. is a parcel courier in Budapest that delivers business senders' parcels across
Hungary. Its site is xlsfutar.hu.

## Numbers

No public page shows an XLS Futár parcel number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows a parcel's status by its parcel number. With no public number to check
its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens numbers from its `id` parameter.
