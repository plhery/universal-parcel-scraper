# In Time Croatia

In Time Croatia parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

In Time is a national parcel courier in Croatia, with its own depots and regional centres, and the
FedEx Express licensee there. Shipments in the FedEx network keep their FedEx numbers. Its site is
in-time.hr. It is a different company from In Time Bulgaria.

## Numbers

Its tracking form gives HR and 12 digits as an example. No public page shows a real number, so there
is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page shows a shipment's origin and destination and a timeline of scans with time,
status and place, up to delivery. With no public number to check its replies against, there is no
adapter.

## Links

The tracking page opens a number from its `id` parameter.
