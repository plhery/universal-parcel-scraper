# NordBrief

NordBrief parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

NordBrief, Nordbrief GmbH of Lübeck, is a regional private postal company on the Baltic coast. It
carries letters, registered mail and parcels. Its site is nordbrief-ostsee.de.

## Numbers

No public page shows a NordBrief number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its parcel portal at paketportal.nordbrief-ostsee.de posts the number from a form. The portal runs
on Hybrilog's parcel portal software, as those of [LVZ Post](../lvz-post/README.md),
[MZZ-Briefdienst](../mzz-briefdienst/README.md) and [PostModern](../post-modern/README.md) do. No
public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

The portal takes the number only through its form, so no address opens one parcel.
