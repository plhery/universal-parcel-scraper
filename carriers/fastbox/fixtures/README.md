# Fixtures

The pages follow `fastbox.co.kr/DHUB/tracking.php?fb_invoice_no={number}`. `in-transit.html` has
Fastbox's own steps, Korea Post's EMS scans and the destination post's, one of them listed twice,
up to the parcel out for delivery, under a shipment table that names the partner and its EMS item
and ends with the recipient's masked name and address. `not-found.html` is the page for an invoice
the tracker does not know: the search form with the invoice and an alert.

Numbers, names, places and clocks are invented.
