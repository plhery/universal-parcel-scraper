# COSHIP

COSHIP parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

COSHIP is the brand of Canada Orient Shipping Ltd. in Canada and 캐나다쉬핑 in Korea. It carries
air courier parcels from Korea to Canada, delivers them itself around Vancouver and Toronto and
hands the others to UPS or Canada Post. Its site is coship.ca.

## Numbers

No public page shows a COSHIP number, so there is no rule. Its ocean shipments are not tracked
online.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page reads `GET www.coship.ca/api/tracking/<number>`, which answers JSON. No public
number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

`www.coship.ca/tracking/?no=<number>` opens one parcel.
