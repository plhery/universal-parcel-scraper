# Ordertracker fixtures

Ordertracker's replies for public numbers, as the endpoint returned them.

- `ups-1Z2231040309026972.json`: Pitney Bowes's and FAR's copies of each UPS scan
  ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20260922043823/https://www.ups.com/WebTracking?loc=en_US&requester=ST&trackNums=1Z2231040309026972)).
- `fedex-541270563758.json`: Mail Boxes Etc's one-line FedEx scans and a pickup dated by its day
  ([source](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6aa6eaabc62ce509075349c1)).
- `canada-post-LA586492541CA.json`: Canada Post's feed, Pitney Bowes's copies and a postal union row
  ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20261005164912/https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/track-reperage/en/search?searchFor=LA586492541CA)).
- `usps-9400136208303575551300.json`: USPS scans through Pitney Bowes, some with the place alone
  ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20261006010859/https://tools.usps.com/go/TrackConfirmAction_input?qtc_tLabels1=9400136208303575551300)).
- `usps-LS020782045US.json`: a delivery relayed with its place alone
  ([source](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6abf5f856239213645935414)).
- `la-poste-6H14479158377.json`: La Poste's wording at real instants
  ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20260928081320/https://www.laposte.fr/outils/suivre-vos-envois?code=6H14479158377)).
- `no-information.json`: the answer kept after Ordertracker failed to read a DHL eCommerce number
  ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20260927160405/https://webtrack.dhlecs.com/?trackingnumber=9261290357482657956985)).
